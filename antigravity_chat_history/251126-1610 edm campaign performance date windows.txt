1. INVESTIGATION: CAMPAIGNS PERFORMANCE MISMATCH (EDM.HTML VS ACTIVECAMPAIGN)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Problem / Context:
  - User reported that the "Campaigns Performance" data in edm.html for date range
    26/10/2026 - 25/11/2026 did not match ActiveCampaign UI metrics.
  - ActiveCampaign reported: 20 campaigns, 530 emails sent, 63.4% open rate,
    9.81% click rate, 15.48% click-to-open, 0.19% bounce rate, 1.51% unsubscribe rate.
  - edm.html displayed: 18 campaigns, 26,012 emails sent, 48.62% open rate,
    11.28% click rate, 1.08% bounce rate, 1.37% unsubscribe rate.

* Root Causes Identified:
  1. Lifetime vs In-Window Aggregation:
     - The legacy `edm-sync` routine pulled `run_campaign_performance_report`
       all-time (with no date filter).
     - `edm.html` filtered rows by `lastSent` matching the selected date window,
       but summed the campaigns' ALL-TIME lifetime metrics (`campSummaryOf()`).
     - 11 of the 18 matching campaigns were `single_automated` (recurring automations)
       whose lifetime multi-year sends (e.g. 6,815, 6,574, 6,066) inflated the
       in-window sends from 530 to 26,012.
  2. Snapshot Staleness:
     - The snapshot in `public.edm_report_metrics` was captured on 2026-11-23.
     - Recent sends from 24-25 Nov 2026 were not included in the weekly cache.
