WHAT "UNCATEGORIZED" MEANS
--------------------------
Any marketing-spend line whose Xero vendor is NOT mapped in
marketing_channel_vendor_map. In the BY_TYPE query the bucket is
coalesce(m.type, 'Uncategorized'). Because the kept-rows rule only retains
unmapped vendors on the OPEN account 302 (Advertising), every Uncategorized
item is a 302 vendor that has not been assigned a type/platform yet.

NOTES
-----
- The spend-by-type chart is FY-aware: on any FY toggle you only see the subset
  whose month falls in that FY (e.g. FY27 -> just Media Management $2,800;
  Belle Property $10,814 is an FY25 item).
- Several look like non-marketing / miscoded charges that landed on 302 — e.g.
  DISNEY, Hoyt(s), aliexpress, Alibaba, Google store, GOOGLE*TV, and literally
  "Global Data SCAM". Since 302 is the open account, these are swept in as
  Uncategorized rather than dropped.
- Some are duplicate spellings of the same vendor (Media Management / Media Mgt;
  Hoyt / Hoyts; Clea Sherman / Clea Writing).
