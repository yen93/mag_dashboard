James's test: every $1 we spend on Google Ads should bring back at least $2 in won revenue (target 2.00×). This is a live view by calendar year — won revenue from the GA4 Leads Tracker (ga4_ac_contact_source) ÷ Google Ads spend (google_ads_campaign_monthly). A “Google Ads” lead = the CRM “Google Ads?” field = Yes, or a google / cpc session; won revenue uses the CRM deal value, counted in the year the enquiry came in. Only years with ad-spend data appear (Jul 2024 onward) — a year with fewer than 12 months of spend (e.g. 2024) reads high (see the note on each year). This is a live cross-check, not the audited ROAS report (which uses ex-GST Buyer Analysis values and stricter attribution).
