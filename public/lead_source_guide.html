Lead Source Guide

Each lead's Conversion Action is the GA4 event that marked them as an enquiry. This guide shows what each one means and which website form(s) it fires on. Links open the live page on myadventuregroup.com.au.

Channels — how the lead arrived

A lead's Channel is the traffic source GA4 attributed to their session, shown as source / medium. GA4 works this out automatically for every visit — you don't set these up inside GA4. Paid and email channels rely on the link being tagged upstream (ad auto-tagging / UTM); referral, direct and organic are detected automatically. The channel is an attribute of the session, carried by the conversion event — not a separate click event.

The channel values in your data

ChannelWhat it means
google / cpcA paid click from a Google Ads ad.
bing / cpcA paid click from a Microsoft / Bing ad.
google / organicA free (unpaid) click from Google search results.
bing / organicA free (unpaid) click from Bing search results.
ActiveCampaign / emailA click from one of your ActiveCampaign emails (the link is tagged utm_source=ActiveCampaign, utm_medium=email).
myadventuregroup.ac-page.com / referralA click through from your own ActiveCampaign-hosted landing / opt-in pages (ac-page.com is AC's hosted-pages domain) — an internal / AC-driven visit, not an external website.
linkedin.com / referralA click from a link on LinkedIn.
teams.public.onecdn.static.microsoft / referralA click from a link opened in Microsoft Teams (its link-preview domain) — usually your link shared in a Teams chat.
(direct) / (none)No source could be detected — the visitor typed the URL, used a bookmark, clicked an untagged link (a PDF, an app, some emails), or the referrer was stripped.

Note: referrals like ads.google.com and tagassistant.google.com are internal / preview-tool artifacts (your own ad interface or Tag Assistant), not real visitors.

Paid search / ads

A paid ad click — e.g. google / cpc, bing / cpc. GA4 reads it from the ad click ID (Google auto-tagging, Microsoft UET) or utm_medium=cpc. These are your Google Ads and Microsoft/Bing Ads clicks.

Organic search

A free, unpaid click from a search engine results page — e.g. google / organic, bing / organic.

Referral

Arrived via a link on another website; GA4 shows the referring domain (e.g. linkedin.com / referral). Heads up: some referrals like ads.google.com and tagassistant.google.com are internal/preview artifacts, not real visitors.

Email

A click from an email link tagged utm_medium=email — e.g. your ActiveCampaign campaigns (ActiveCampaign / email).

Direct / unknown

No source could be detected — the visitor typed the URL, used a bookmark, or the referrer was stripped. GA4 labels this (direct) / (none).

Social

A click from a social network such as LinkedIn or Facebook.

Video

A click from a video platform such as YouTube (youtube / video).

Other

Any other source/medium GA4 detected automatically from UTM tags, the referring site, or the ad click ID.

How the Match & Confidence are decided

Calendly (email) — the booking's email also exists as an AC contact (exact identity). Always high.

GA4 (time) — matched by timestamp: a GA4 form event fired within ±5 minutes of the contact's AC creation time. Confidence then depends on how close (Δ seconds) and whether the minute was ambiguous (more than one possible source):

highOnly one possible source in the window and within 90 seconds.
mediumUnambiguous but 90–300s away, or within 90s but several possible sources.
lowSeveral possible sources and 90–300s away.
noneNo source could be matched (unmatched).
