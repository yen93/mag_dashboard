gate the page doesn't: GADS_FILTER = (google_ads_ac_field='Yes' OR channel='google /

='No', channel null). So ROAS counts 3; the page shows all 5. Explained it's by
design: ROAS is a Google-Ads-attributed cross-check, and the two dropped rows are an
organic deal and a "Could be SEO or Paid Ad" audit row — neither a confirmed Google
Ads win. Nothing broken.
